Guan-Ting Liu

Guan-Ting (Danny) Liu is a research scientist in NVIDIA Design Automation Research Group. He completed his Ph.D. in the Graduate Institute of Networking and Multimedia at National Taiwan University in Taipei, Taiwan. During his Ph.D. program, he is advised by Pu-Jen Cheng, Iris Hui-Ru Jiang, and Shao-Hua Sun. His research focuses on Reinforcement Learning, Machine Learning, and applying ML techniques for Design Automation Problems. Before entering the graduate school, he finished his bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering with cross-discipline in Computer Science at NCTU (NYCU by now).

Research

I'm interested in Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning(RL), and Electronic Design Automation (EDA). Much of my research is about interpretability and generalization of RL and applying machine learning techniques to solve EDA problems.
